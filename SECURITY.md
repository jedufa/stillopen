# Security

Experimental and unmaintained. No monitored disclosure channel or guaranteed security updates are offered. Do not rely on this snapshot for sensitive records without an independent review.

App storage is ordinary preferences/files, not an encrypted vault. Backups and shares can disclose titles and attachments, and notification titles can appear on a lock screen. There is no bundled hosted service. Avoid placing secrets or private records in public issues, logs or contributions. Future forks should define their own supported versions and disclosure process.
